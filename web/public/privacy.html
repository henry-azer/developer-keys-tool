Developer Keys

Privacy

Last updated 15 October 2026

Your vault stays on your PC

Developer Keys runs on your own computer. Your keys, passwords and tokens are encrypted in your browser with AES-256 before they're saved, using a key made from your master password. The encrypted vault is stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Developer Keys.

Nobody can read it without your master password, including the developer. There is no account and no recovery.

What leaves your PC

  • Nothing, by default. Developer Keys has no server, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Smart add (optional) appears only when the Claude Code app is installed and signed in on your PC. When you press “Map with Claude”, the text you pasted is sent to Anthropic through your own Claude Code sign-in, under Anthropic’s terms. Nothing is sent unless you press it.
  • Share files and backups are written to your Documents folder. Where they go next is up to you.

Microsoft Teams

The Developer Keys tab is loaded from https://localhost:53917 on your PC. Teams only knows that you opened the app; it never receives your vault.

Backups

Automatic backup keeps an encrypted copy in Documents\Developer Keys Backups. It opens with your master password. You can turn it off in Settings.
